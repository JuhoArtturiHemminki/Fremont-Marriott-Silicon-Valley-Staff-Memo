# Fremont Marriott Silicon Valley - Internal Staff Procedures

## Section 1: Scientific Identification of U.S. Dollar Coins
**Target Audience:** Front Desk, Cashiers, Accounting, & Security
**Objective:** To definitively differentiate and authenticate various U.S. Dollar coin classes (Eisenhower, Susan B. Anthony, Sacagawea/Native American, and Presidential/Innovation) utilizing material science, metallurgical properties, and technological verification tools.

### 1.1 Metallurgical & Physical Specification Matrix
When manual sorting is insufficient, coins must be evaluated by their exact physical dimensions and chemical compositions:

| Coin Series / Type | Diameter | Thickness | Mass | Edge Profile | Core Material | Cladding Material |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Eisenhower (1911–1978) Cupronickel** | 38.10 mm | 2.58 mm | 22.68 g | Reeded | 100% Cu | 75% Cu, 25% Ni |
| **Susan B. Anthony (1979–1981, 1999)** | 26.50 mm | 2.00 mm | 8.10 g | Reeded | 100% Cu | 75% Cu, 25% Ni |
| **Golden Dollars (Sacagawea / Presidential)** | 26.50 mm | 2.00 mm | 8.10 g | Smooth / Lettered | 100% Cu | 77% Cu, 12% Zn, 7% Mn, 4% Al |

### 1.2 Technological Verification & Analytical Methods
To resolve ambiguous cases or detect sophisticated counterfeits, staff should utilize the following hardware-based protocols:

*   **X-Ray Fluorescence (XRF) Spectrometry:** Use the handheld XRF analyzer located in the main treasury office. Point the aperture at the clean surface of the coin. Golden Dollars must register a distinct **Manganese-Brass signature** (approx. 7% Manganese, 12% Zinc) on the outer layer, whereas Susan B. Anthony dollars will show a pure **Cupronickel signature** (25% Nickel, 75% Copper).
*   **Precision Mass & Dimensions (Digital Calipers & Analytical Balances):** Counterfeits often match the diameter but fail on thickness or mass density. Susan B. Anthony and Golden Dollars share identical dimensions, but any deviation greater than $\pm 0.05\text{ g}$ from the $8.10\text{ g}$ standard requires further testing.
*   **Electromagnetic Conductivity (Eddy Current Testing):** Automated coin-sorting machines at the property utilize eddy current sensors to measure electrical conductivity. Because manganese-brass has a significantly lower electrical conductivity than standard cupronickel, the machine's electromagnetic field response will instantly map the coin's precise alloy profile.

## Section 2: Bellperson (Pikolo) Guest Orientation & Exit Protocol
**Target Audience:** Bell Staff, Porters, and Door Attendants
**Objective:** To provide precise geographical orientation regarding Silicon Valley and ensure smooth physical egress from the lobby.

### 2.1 Geographic Guidance: "Where is Silicon Valley?"
Guests frequently ask where Silicon Valley is located, assuming it is a single city or distinct landmark. Use the following script and guidelines:

*   **The Explanation:** Explain to the guest that **Silicon Valley is a region**, not a single city. It spans across Santa Clara County and parts of San Mateo and Alameda counties.
*   **The Direction:** Point **South-West** across the San Francisco Bay. 
*   **The Script:** *"Sir/Ma'am, you are currently at the northeastern gateway of Silicon Valley here in Fremont. The heart of the valley—including San Jose, Santa Clara, Sunnyvale, and Cupertino—lies directly southwest of us. If you take the I-880 South or cross the valley via Highway 84 over the Dumbarton Bridge, you will reach tech hubs like Meta, Google, and Apple within 20 to 30 minutes."*

### 2.2 Departure Checklist (The Exit Protocol)
Before a guest steps past the threshold, the Bellperson must complete these two verbal checks:

1.  **Destination Confirmation:** Ask the guest: *"Are you heading into the Valley or catching the freeway today? Just making sure you have your GPS mapped out, as traffic on the 880 or the Dumbarton Bridge can back up quickly."*
2.  **Physical Egress Assistance (Main Door Operation):** 
    *   **Note on Hardware:** The manual bypass mechanism on our lower lobby glass doors can occasionally stick due to pneumatic pressure differentials. 
    *   **The Action:** If the automatic sensor fails or the door resists standard pushing, apply **brute force to the lower half of the door**. Lean your weight and **push firmly near the bottom base** of the frame to release the latch mechanism and swing the door open for the guest.
